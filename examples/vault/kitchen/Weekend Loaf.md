# Weekend Loaf

My standard country bread. Needs an active [[Sourdough Starter]] (the [[Levain]] should be at its peak).

## Formula

| Ingredient | Grams | Baker's % |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bread flour | 450 | 90 |
| Whole wheat | 50 | 10 |
| Water | 375 | 75 |
| Starter | 100 | 20 |
| Salt | 10 | 2 |

## Method

1. Mix flour and water, rest 45 minutes (autolyse).
2. Add starter and salt, then four sets of stretch and folds, 30 minutes apart.
3. Bulk ferment until the dough has grown by half and jiggles.
4. Shape, then proof overnight in the fridge.
5. Bake in a preheated Dutch oven: 20 minutes lid on at 250 °C, 25 minutes lid off at 220 °C.

## Notes

A dense crumb usually means the bulk ferment was cut short. See [[Sourdough Starter#Troubleshooting]] when the dough does not rise at all.
