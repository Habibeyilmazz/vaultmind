# Sourdough Starter

A starter is a culture of wild yeast and lactic acid bacteria living in flour and water. Mine is called Doris and lives in a glass jar on the counter.

## Feeding schedule

Feed once a day at room temperature: discard all but 20 g, then add 40 g of flour and 40 g of water. In the fridge it only needs feeding once a week. If a grey liquid collects on top, the culture is hungry; pour it off and feed twice in a row.

## Signs it is ready

The starter is ready to bake with when it has doubled in four to six hours, smells pleasantly sour, and a spoonful floats in water. Use it at the peak, just before it starts to fall.

## Troubleshooting

- Sluggish in winter: move the jar somewhere warmer, around 26 °C.
- Pink or orange streaks: throw it away and start again.
- Too sour: feed more often and use a stiffer mix.
