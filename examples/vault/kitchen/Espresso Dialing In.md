# Espresso Dialing In

Dialing in means adjusting grind, dose and yield until the shot tastes balanced.

## Baseline recipe

18 g of ground coffee in, 36 g of liquid out, in 27 to 32 seconds. Change one variable at a time.

## Reading the taste

- Sour, thin, fast shot: under-extracted. Grind finer.
- Bitter, harsh, slow shot: over-extracted. Grind coarser.
- Both sour and bitter: channeling. Fix the puck preparation before touching the grinder.

## Milk drinks

For a café crème or a flat white I pull a slightly shorter shot (1:1.8) so the coffee cuts through the milk.

Beans from the roaster I visited on the [[Lisbon Trip]] behaved very differently; lighter roasts need a finer grind and hotter water.
