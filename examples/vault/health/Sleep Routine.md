# Sleep Routine

## Rules

1. No caffeine after 13:00.
2. Screens off at 22:00, lights dimmed.
3. Bedroom at 18 °C.
4. Up at 06:30 every day, weekends included.

## Why

Based on [[Why We Sleep]]. Since following these rules I fall asleep within fifteen minutes on most nights, and my [[Running Log]] shows better recovery.
