# Make It Stick

Book notes. The central claim: learning that feels easy is often shallow, and effortful retrieval is what makes knowledge durable.

## Key ideas

- **Retrieval practice**: testing yourself beats rereading.
- **Spacing**: leave gaps between sessions so that recall takes effort.
- **Interleaving**: mix problem types instead of practising one in a block.
- **Generation**: try to solve a problem before being shown the answer.

## What I changed

I stopped highlighting. After each chapter I close the book and write down what I remember, then check. Details of my review system are in [[Spaced Repetition]].

Sleep matters too: memories are consolidated overnight, see [[Why We Sleep]].
