# Why We Sleep

Book notes on the science of sleep.

## Key ideas

- Deep sleep early in the night moves new facts into long term storage.
- REM sleep late in the night connects ideas and softens emotional memories.
- Caffeine has a half life of five to six hours; an afternoon coffee is still in the system at bedtime.
- A cool, dark bedroom and a fixed wake up time help more than any gadget.
