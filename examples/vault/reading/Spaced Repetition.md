# Spaced Repetition

Reviewing a fact just before you would forget it. Each successful recall pushes the next review further into the future.

## My setup

- Flashcards are written as questions, one fact per card.
- New cards: at most ten per day.
- Reviews happen with morning coffee and take about ten minutes.

## Intervals

A card I get right is shown again after 1, 3, 7, 16 and 35 days. A card I get wrong goes back to one day.

## Why it works

Forgetting is steep at first and then flattens. Recalling at the edge of forgetting is hard, and that difficulty is the point, as argued in [[Make It Stick]].
