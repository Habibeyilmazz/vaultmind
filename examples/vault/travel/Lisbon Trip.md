# Lisbon Trip

Four days in April.

## Itinerary

- Day 1: Alfama on foot, sunset from a miradouro
- Day 2: Tram to Belém, custard tarts still warm from the oven
- Day 3: Day trip to Sintra, palaces and fog
- Day 4: Coffee roaster tour, then the tile museum

## Practical

Buy a rechargeable transit card at the airport metro station. Hills are steep; wear shoes with grip. Most museums are closed on Mondays.

## Brought home

Two bags of lightly roasted beans, which sent me back to [[Espresso Dialing In]].
