# Garden Irrigation

Drip lines for the raised beds, switched by a single solenoid valve.

## Watering rules

Tomatoes and peppers get 30 minutes at dawn. The valve stays shut if the [[Weather Station]] measured more than 4 mm of rain in the last 24 hours, or if the forecast promises rain before noon.

## Parts

- 16 mm main line, 4 mm drippers every 30 cm
- 12 V latching solenoid valve, so it only draws power while switching
- Pressure reducer set to 1.5 bar

## Lessons

Mulch matters more than any schedule: beds covered with straw needed about half the water.
